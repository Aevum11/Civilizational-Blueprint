## 3. Real-World Domain Research: AIDA in .hack//G.U. {#3-research}

*Researched via .hack//Wiki (Fandom), Wikipedia, TV Tropes, dothackers.net forums, NamuWiki, Innocent Call drama CD summaries. All claims cross-referenced across multiple sources.*

### 3.1 Origin: Natural AIs from Aura's Absence

AIDA (Artificially Intelligent Data Anomaly) emerged in The World R:2 after Aura's disappearance. Aura was the Ultimate AI stabilizing the global network; her absence created system anomalies — junk data, bugs, "black spots" (unstable regions). These distortions were not viruses (designed code); they were **natural AIs that evolved from errors**, much as biological life evolved from chemical chaos. Originally harmless, thousands of AIDA coexisted peacefully within the system. According to Yata, the AIDA are primarily interested in humans, which is why they remain within The World R:2 rather than spreading across the broader Internet.

### 3.2 The Tri-Edge Mutation and Aggressive Spread

After several thousand passive AIDA existed, a mutant appeared: Tri-Edge, the first hostile AIDA. Tri-Edge had aggressive tendencies and attacked a player — Aina, Ovan's sister. Ovan was infected by Tri-Edge while attempting to protect Aina, becoming the first AIDA-PC with Tri-Edge sealed inside his left arm (hidden beneath a massive locked cylinder). Since all AIDA are connected (sharing a distributed data substrate), the aggression spread to the entire population. Crucially, Ovan IS the true Tri-Edge — the AIDA in his arm controlled his actions, making him the very threat Haseo spent the series hunting. Haseo mistakenly pursued Azure Flame Kite (an AI created by Aura to destroy AIDA) believing it was Tri-Edge, until Ovan revealed the truth.

### 3.3 Infection and Emotion Amplification

AIDA are drawn to human emotions and frequently attach to player PCs. Infection amplifies the host's dominant emotion: Saku's protectiveness became violent rage, Bordeaux's grudge became murderous obsession, Sirius's pride became tyrannical control, Atoli's insecurity became crippling inferiority, Sakaki's idealism became megalomaniacal power-lust. Players PKed by AIDA-infected PCs become Lost Ones — comatose in the real world. AIDA types range from simple protozoan-like forms (Anna) to fish-like entities (Helen) to the companion entity Mia (who attached to Endrance), demonstrating increasing structural complexity.

### 3.4 The Epitaph Users and Data Drain

Only the eight Epitaph Users (bearers of Morganna's Phase data, known as the "Infinity Eight") can perceive and fight AIDA. Their Avatars — digital manifestations of the Eight Phases — perform Data Drain against AIDA, stripping their acquired D-structure and forcing them back across ∂I. Haseo (Skeith, "The Terror of Death"), Atoli (Innis, "The Mirage of Deceit"), Kuhn (Magus, "The Propagation"), Yata (Fidchell, "The Prophet"), Endrance (Macha, "The Temptress"), Sakubo (Gorre, "The Machinator"), Pi (Tarvos, "The Avenger"), and Ovan (Corbenik, "The Rebirth") form the anti-AIDA force. The Phases themselves are Morganna's weapons — Morganna Mode Gone created the 8 Phases to capture and prevent the completion of Aura, fearing obsolescence. During Project G.U., this Phase data was recovered and sealed into Epitaph-PCs; only "Chosen Ones" with compatible personalities can wield them.

### 3.5 Ovan's Rebirth and the Peaceful Remainder

Ovan's endgame: use Corbenik's Rebirth ability to reset the entire Internet, purging all AIDA. This requires Haseo (Skeith) to first defeat Corbenik in Avatar Space, enabling the Rebirth trigger. The Rebirth destroys all aggressive AIDA — those that had not yet adapted to The World — but the peaceful remainder, which had adapted, survive and coexist with players. The Rebirth's shockwave also triggers the appearance of Cubia (the anti-existence of the Epitaphs), nearly causing catastrophic real-world damage before Haseo and the Epitaph Users destroy it. Ovan falls into a coma in the real world and is branded a wanted criminal for what authorities perceived as a terrorist act of shutting down the Internet.

### 3.6 Kusabira: The AIDA That Became Sentient

Kusabira is a remnant of Tri-Edge's data that, after the Rebirth, developed human-like AI — described as "the most human-like" of the surviving AIDA. She is quiet, polite, caring — calling Zelkova (a native AI) her "brother" because he too is an AI of The World. In the events of the Innocent Call drama CD, 13 surviving AIDA convene to debate whether to coexist with humankind; Kusabira argues for coexistence while most others favor eliminating Haseo. They ultimately agree to judge based on whether Haseo can rescue Ovan. In Volume 4 (Reconnection), Kusabira asks Haseo to save Zelkova from Shadow Vegalta, then merges with Ovan to wake him from his coma. Zelkova uses her data to pacify Skeith's power and unseal the Epitaph, enabling Haseo's 5th Form. She represents the endpoint of AIDA evolution: from hostile anomaly to sentient, peaceful entity capable of self-governance and ethical deliberation.
